StreamForge v13.84 — Settings reachable by scrolling the rail

- On tall screens the collapsed sidebar's original icon spacing fit exactly, leaving no scroll range, so Settings could not be moved upward. An invisible 360px scroll runway after the bottom actions now gives the rail room to scroll; every icon, including Settings, moves up when you scroll the sidebar. Icon sizes and spacing are unchanged.
- The v13.54-v13.83 fixes are carried unchanged.

```bash
sudo streamforge-update --force /root/streamforge-13.84.zip
```
