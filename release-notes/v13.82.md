StreamForge v13.82 — compact collapsed sidebar

- The collapsed desktop sidebar rail spent 48px per icon, so the 15 links plus the bottom actions overflowed a normal viewport and icons were pushed out of view. Icons now use 34px each, with a shorter brand/toggle and bottom actions — everything fits at the top on a normal screen. The sidebar still scrolls as a fallback on very short screens.
- The v13.54-v13.81 fixes are carried unchanged.

```bash
sudo streamforge-update --force /root/streamforge-13.82.zip
```
