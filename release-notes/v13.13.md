StreamForge v13.13 — visible notification and update controls

- Centers Change password and Sign out against the complete 76px collapsed sidebar.
- Adds a prominent **Server time zone** panel at the top of Notification Center with its own Save action.
- Opens sent Email/SMS subject and body in an accessible modal popup.
- Shows **Update processing** immediately after Main Update is submitted and polls for the matching helper request result.
- Groups Playlist User email, mobile, Email notification and SMS notification controls together with clear independent on/off checkboxes.

Requires installed Main **v13.04** or newer. Remote Nodes do not require a runtime update.

```bash
sudo streamforge-update --force /root/streamforge-13.13.zip
```
